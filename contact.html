Research teams

Teams at NUS Medicine and NUHS can approach the Lab at any stage, though the earlier the better: involvement shapes a study most usefully before the design is fixed, and it can be costed into a grant application only if it is planned before submission. Tell us what the study is, where it has got to, and what you think you need.

Patients, caregivers, and members of the public

People interested in contributing to research are welcome to get in touch. No background in research is needed. Remuneration arrangements for contributors are being settled in line with national guidance now in development.
