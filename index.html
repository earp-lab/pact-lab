PACT Lab

Participation, meaningfully and systematically

We are building an ecosystem where patients, families, caregivers, and members of the public can participate meaningfully and systematically in healthcare research and service transformation.

PACT Lab will connect lived experience with academic expertise and real-world health-system practice. Through this partnership, we aim to strengthen the relevance, accessibility and impact of research and healthcare improvement.

Why PACT Lab?

Knowledge grounded in lived experience

Patients, families, caregivers and members of the public bring knowledge grounded in lived experience. Their perspectives can guide teams to:

  • Identify priorities that matter to people and communities
  • Shape research questions and service design
  • Improve feasibility, accessibility and inclusion
  • Interpret findings in ways that reflect real-world experience
  • Ground the implementation and evaluation of change

PACT Lab is being established to provide a shared platform for meaningful and sustainable partnership, supported by common standards, practical methods, appropriate remuneration, capability-building and evaluation.

What PACT Lab will do

Five areas of work

Support meaningful partnerships

Connect patients, families, caregivers and members of the public with research and service-transformation teams.

Develop shared standards and methods

Create practical guidance covering participation, accessibility, remuneration, project support and evaluation.

Build capability

Equip patient and public partners, researchers, clinicians and service-transformation teams with the knowledge and skills to work together effectively.

Support demonstrator projects

Work intensively with selected healthcare research and service-transformation projects to test and improve the PACT Lab model.

Generate and share learning

Evaluate how partnership influences decisions, participant experiences, research, healthcare services and organisational practice.

Where are we now?

Establishing the foundations

PACT Lab is currently establishing the infrastructure needed to support meaningful, inclusive and sustainable patient and public partnership. Our initial work includes:

  • Establishing the governance and operating model
  • Recruiting the core team
  • Mapping existing participation activities, capabilities, networks and gaps
  • Developing participation, remuneration and accessibility principles
  • Forming a Patient and Public Leadership Network
  • Developing practical tools and training
  • Identifying initial research and service-transformation demonstrators
  • Establishing an evaluation framework to understand experience, influence and impact
