Governance

Jointly led, by design

PACT is governed jointly by its three partners. Patients and the public shape the platform itself, in addition to advising the research it supports.

Centre for Biomedical Ethics, NUS Medicine

Methodological, ethical, and empirical expertise: the standards and justifications on which credible PPIE depends, alongside hands-on capability including public consultation, survey work, and the Health Opinion Panel Singapore (HOPS).

NUHS Group Chief Patient Officer

System-wide strategy and delivery of patient and caregiver experience, advocacy, and partnership across NUHS institutions, and alignment with research and service transformation priorities. Operational reach and clinical alignment: connection to NUHS institutions, patient and family partnership structures, and live research and service priorities.

NUHS Patient & Family Partners

Co-leadership by patient and public partners, so that the platform's priorities, procedures, and culture are set with the people it exists to involve.
