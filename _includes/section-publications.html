Foundations and justification

Why patients and the public should be involved in research, and on what grounds. Work on the ethics, justification, and governance of involvement, including the question of what follows when the usual instrumental arguments do not apply.

Methods

How involvement is done, and how it is studied. Includes Collective Reflective Equilibrium in Practice (CREP), the framework PACT uses to move from public and patient input to justifiable policy and translation recommendations, and the empirical and experimental methods the Lab draws on.

Studies and applications

Involvement in practice: surveys of public attitudes, qualitative and deliberative work with patients and the public, and co-design of research and services.
