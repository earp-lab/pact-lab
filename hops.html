What HOPS is for

HOPS serves questions that do not require experience of a particular condition: study design, participant-facing materials, consent and recruitment approaches, and public attitudes to health research, technologies, and policy. Panel members are recruited to be representative of the Singapore population by age, sex, ethnicity, and other demographic characteristics, and are paid for their time.

For questions that do require experience of a particular condition, HOPS is not the right route. PACT brokers access to condition-specific patient groups instead, through clinical teams, patient support organisations, and existing patient panels across NUS Medicine and NUHS.

Access

HOPS is a finite resource. Members were recruited on the understanding that they would be invited to take part a limited number of times each year, and that commitment governs how much of the panel is available. Access is therefore allocated rather than granted on request, and the Lab publishes the criteria it applies.

Requests are reviewed for fit, for the quality of the instrument, and for capacity. Support with survey design is part of what the Lab provides, since a poorly designed instrument spends panel capacity without producing usable evidence.

Access criteria, capacity, and charges are being finalised and will be published here.
